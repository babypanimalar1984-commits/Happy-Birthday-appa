From your son, with all my heart

Dear Dad,

I don't think I say this enough, so today I want to say it properly.

Thank you, Dad.

Thank you for every morning you wake up and go to work. Thank you for every long day, every late night, and every weekend you spend working for us. There are so many things you do that we may not always notice, but they are all around us.

Sometimes you don't get enough time to sit with us. Sometimes your work takes away the evenings, the weekends, and the little moments that families wish they had more of.

But Dad, I want you to know something.

We notice your sacrifice.

We notice how hard you work. We notice how much you care. We notice how you keep going even when you're tired.

You never ask us for anything in return. You simply work, provide, protect, and love us in your own way.

And maybe one day, when I am older, I will finally understand just how much you gave up for our family. But even now, I know one thing for certain:

You mean the world to us.

I don't want this birthday to be remembered as just another day.

I want you to remember that your son loves you. Your family loves you. And behind every little thing we have today, there is a part of your hard work and your love.

I may not always say it. I may not always show it. But I am proud to call you my Dad.

I hope this little page stays with you as a reminder that your efforts have never been meaningless.

One day, I want to give you back even a small part of everything you have given us.

Until then, please know that every long day you work is remembered, every sacrifice is appreciated, and every moment you spend caring for us is loved.

Happy Birthday, Dad.
Thank you for being our strength.
Thank you for being our home.
Thank you for being you.

With all my love,
Your Son ❤️
